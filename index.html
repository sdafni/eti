Eti

Tap a message, get a calendar plan or a to-do. Eti reads the date, time, place and who it's with, and saves it to Google Calendar or Tasks.

Download for Android

How it works

  1. Tap the small floating button. It turns into a calendar with a pen.
  2. Tap a message in WhatsApp, or text in any other app. Eti circles it.
  3. Pick Plan or To-do, check the form, and save.

Install

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open it. Allow your browser to install apps when asked.
  2. Open Eti and follow its setup: make Eti the phone's digital assistant (with "Use text from screen" and "Use screenshot" on), and let it display over other apps.
  3. Connect your Google account for Calendar and Tasks.

Good to know

Eti reads the screen only when you tap a message. That text, or a screenshot of it, is sent to Anthropic's Claude to work out the plan, and is not kept.

Eti is an early preview. Connecting Google works for invited accounts only for now. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Android 8 or later. New builds appear here automatically.
